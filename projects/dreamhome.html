Description

Findit Real Estate is a collaborative web development project created as part of the Netlinks Web Development Bootcamp Challenge. Our team was given a reference website and challenged to recreate it through our own implementation. We worked together to build the page structure, responsive layouts, styling, and visual details while maintaining the overall direction of the reference design.

My Contribution

As a Front-End Developer and team member, I contributed to the planning, HTML structure, CSS styling, layout implementation, and refinement of different sections of the website. I worked closely with the team to maintain consistency across the interface and helped bring the final design together.
